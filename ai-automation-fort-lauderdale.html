AI Automation: Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI automation for a small business?

AI automation uses software and AI models to handle repetitive tasks, like replying to new leads, answering common questions, sending reminders, and moving data between apps, without someone doing it by hand.

Will AI replace my staff or sound robotic?

No. The goal is to take busywork off your team's plate. We write messages in your brand voice, keep a human in the loop for important conversations, and make it easy for customers to reach a real person.

How much does AI automation cost?

Pricing depends on how many workflows you need and which tools you already use. Many businesses start with one or two high-impact automations. We'll give you a clear quote after a free consultation.

Do I need new software?

Not always. We often build on the tools you already pay for, like your email, calendar, CRM, or booking app. If a new tool makes sense, we'll explain why and what it costs.

Do you offer AI automation outside Fort Lauderdale?

Yes. We're based in Fort Lauderdale and meet in person across Broward County, and we set up automations remotely for businesses throughout South Florida and the U.S.
