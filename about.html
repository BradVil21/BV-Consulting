A Fort Lauderdale Team Helping Small Businesses Win Online

Our story

From fixing tech problems to building growth systems

BV Consulting started in Fort Lauderdale helping homes and small businesses with hands-on tech support. Working side by side with local owners, we kept seeing the same pattern: great businesses losing customers to missed calls, outdated websites nobody could find on Google, and hours lost to copy-and-paste admin work.

So we focused on fixing the bigger problem. Today BV Consulting designs SEO-ready websites, builds AI automations, and connects business apps with API integrations, all as one system that helps small businesses get found, respond faster, and grow.

We still bring the same approach that earned trust from day one: honest advice, clear pricing, and explanations in plain English.
