Frequently Asked Questions

What does BV Consulting do?

BV Consulting is a Fort Lauderdale, FL agency that builds small businesses a complete online growth system: a custom website designed to rank on Google, AI automation that responds to and follows up with every lead, and API integrations that connect the apps you already use.

How much does a small business website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, features, and integrations you need. After a free consultation we give you a clear, written quote with no hidden fees. For typical market ranges, read our 2026 website cost guide.

What tasks can AI automate for my business?

Common wins include instant replies to new leads, missed-call text back, website chat that answers common questions, appointment booking and reminders, review requests after a job, and follow-up emails. See our AI automation services for more.

What is an API integration and do I need one?

An API integration lets two apps share data automatically, for example sending website leads straight into your CRM or syncing payments to QuickBooks. If your team copies the same information between apps, an integration will likely save you time. Learn more on our API integrations page.

Do you only work with businesses in Fort Lauderdale?

We're based in Fort Lauderdale and love meeting local owners in person anywhere in Broward County. Because websites and automations are built online, we also work with small businesses across South Florida and the rest of the U.S.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope, and your proposal will include a clear timeline. As a rough guide, a small business website usually takes a few weeks, while a single automation or integration can often go live much faster.
