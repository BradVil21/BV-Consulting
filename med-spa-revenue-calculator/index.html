The math

How the Calculator Works

No hidden assumptions. Here's exactly how each number is calculated.

Unanswered lead revenue

Missed inquiries per week × 4.33 weeks × share that would book × average visit value

Every call, text, or DM that goes unanswered is a potential client who may book somewhere else. 4.33 is the average number of weeks in a month.

No-show revenue

Appointments per month × no-show & late cancel rate × average visit value

Once an appointment time passes, that chair can't be resold, so each no-show or late cancel is revenue the day can't get back.

Recovery scenario

(Unanswered lead revenue ÷ 3) + (No-show revenue × 25%)

A conservative what-if, not a guarantee: winning back one in three missed leads with instant replies and cutting no-shows by a quarter with reminders.

Yearly estimate

Monthly total × 12

Seasonal swings change the real number, so treat the yearly figure as a rough picture of what's at stake.

FAQ

Revenue Calculator FAQ

Is this calculator free?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser, and nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.

How accurate is the estimate?

It's an illustrative estimate based on the numbers you enter. Real results depend on your services, pricing, policies, and how quickly your team follows up with new inquiries.

What counts as a late cancellation?

Most practices count any cancellation inside their cancellation policy window, often 24 to 48 hours before the appointment, because that time is hard to refill.

How can a med spa lower its no-show rate?

Confirmation texts with a simple reply to confirm or reschedule, reminders a day or two ahead, deposits for longer appointments, and waitlist texts that refill openings all help. See our SMS and booking automation service.
