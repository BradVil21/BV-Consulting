Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy is provided for general informational purposes and is not legal advice. Please review it with a qualified attorney to make sure it fits your business and complies with the laws that apply to you.

BV Consulting ("BV Consulting," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit bvconsulting.live or use our services, how we use that information, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide may include:

  • Your name, business name, phone number, email address, website, and city when you request a consultation or contact us.
  • Details about your business, goals, and the software tools you use.
  • Account access, credentials, or data you share with us so we can build your website, automations, or integrations.

Information collected automatically may include your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and similar usage data gathered through cookies and analytics tools such as Google Analytics.

2. How we use your information

  • Respond to consultation requests, questions, and project inquiries.
  • Plan, build, and support the websites, automations, and integrations you hire us for.
  • Communicate with you about your project, including follow-ups and updates.
  • Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers only as needed to run our business, for example: form delivery services (such as FormSubmit) that email your submission to us, website hosting, analytics (such as Google Analytics), scheduling, and communication tools. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety, rights, or property of BV Consulting, our clients, or others.

4. Client data in projects

When we build automations or integrations, we may access data stored in your business systems (such as your CRM, calendar, or accounting software). We access only what's needed to deliver the agreed work, use it only for that purpose, and follow reasonable security practices. Where AI tools process your data, we choose providers and settings appropriate for business use and will discuss this with you during your project.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use our site. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. When it's no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

7. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete your information, subject to legal requirements. You can opt out of marketing messages by following the unsubscribe instructions or contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

9. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Third-party links

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do.
