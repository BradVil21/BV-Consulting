FAQ

Med Spa SMS & Booking Automation: Frequently Asked Questions

Can automated texts reduce no-shows?

Reminders give clients an easy way to confirm or reschedule, which helps practices recover slots they would otherwise lose. Combined with a clear deposit or cancellation policy, they're one of the most practical ways to protect your schedule.

Will this work with my booking software?

Most med spa booking platforms offer built-in reminders, integrations, or an API. We review your platform, use native features where they're strong, and add automations where they're not.

What is business texting registration?

U.S. carriers require businesses that send texts through software from local numbers to register their brand and message use (often called A2P 10DLC). Registration helps your texts get delivered instead of filtered.

Can we text marketing promotions?

Yes, with the right consent. Promotional texts require clear opt-in and easy opt-out, and federal and state rules apply. We build consent capture into your forms and booking flow, and recommend legal review of your policy.

Do texts include treatment details?

We keep messages minimal by default, such as your practice name, date, and time, to protect client privacy. Any more detailed messaging is set up deliberately with your compliance guidance.
